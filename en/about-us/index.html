Thirty-five years in the same craft, and still not bored

We started when the internet in Italy was a curiosity and websites were written by hand. Since then we have lived through every technological fashion and learned to spot which ones actually mattered. Artificial intelligence, this time, actually matters.

Antonio De Donno, founder

I started building websites when connections were measured in kilobits and nobody knew what a search engine was. Since then I have seen everything come and go: portals, the e-commerce boom, social media, mobile, and now artificial intelligence. I have learned exactly one thing, and it applies to all of them: technology is worth having when it removes a problem for the people doing the work. If it adds one, however elegant it is, it is wasted time. That is the criterion I use to decide what to propose to clients and what to advise against, even when advising against means invoicing less.

Antonio De Donno — Founder & CTO

The team

Behind Inginet there is a group of people chosen one at a time: backend and frontend developers, people who work on data and AI, interface designers, people who look after content and translation in the five languages, people who shoot photo and video, people who run campaigns. Nobody hands your project to someone else halfway: whoever starts with you, finishes with you.

What we work with

We pick technology by the problem, not by fashion: static sites generated where maximum speed and security matter, PHP/Laravel and Node.js applications for management systems, relational databases, language models connected to company data for the AI features, infrastructure on European servers. And we maintain what already exists, WordPress included, when rebuilding is not worth it.

How we think

The problem first, the technology after

Nobody has ever called us because they wanted “some software”. They call because they are losing hours, losing orders or losing customers. That is where we start.

We say no as well

If a request makes no sense for your business we say so, with reasons. A wrong project accepted is a client lost the following year.

Simple things are the hardest

A panel an employee can use without a training course takes more effort than one crammed with features. That effort is worth making.

We stay reachable

Direct number, WhatsApp, email. When something breaks you don’t need a ticket portal: you need someone who answers.

Frequently asked questions

Where are you and can I visit?

The office is in Maglie, in the province of Lecce, southern Italy. You are very welcome, ideally by appointment so we can take the time to talk properly. Clients further away we look after by video call with no difference in quality.

Who will actually handle my project?

One fixed point of contact for the whole job, backed by the specialists it needs. You are not passed between departments and you never start explaining from scratch again.

Why does a software house also do photo and video?

Because well-built software with poor visual material does not sell. Handling it directly means we don’t depend on third parties for timing and can deliver work that is coherent from start to finish.

Tell us the problem, we’ll tell you how we solve it

Free initial analysis, no strings attached. We reply within one working day, in English, Italian, German, French or Spanish.
