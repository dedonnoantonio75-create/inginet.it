Case study · Style Sport 2

Nexora, the retail management software born at Style Sport 2

For Style Sport 2, a fishing, boating and diving shop, we did not just build the online store: we also wrote the management software that runs the shop. It is called Nexora and it was born right there, between the counter and the stockroom, to keep till, stock, suppliers, website and eBay on the same data.

  • Fast checkout with receipts, invoices, returns and gift cards
  • One stock with sizes, colours, variants and EAN labels
  • Supplier orders, invoices from the Italian SDI system and delivery notes read by AI
  • Six-language website and eBay linked to real stock levels
  • E-invoicing, registers, deadlines and books balanced to the cent

Today Nexora also works for other shops and businesses: we adapt it to the way you sell.

Our own platform · Olvix

Olvix, the marketplace we built from scratch

Olvix is the national platform we designed and wrote ourselves, from the first module to the last. Stays, rentals, chauffeur services, auctions, restaurants, shops and events run on the same system, each with its own management software. In the video, the module for restaurants and pizzerias.

  • The menu loads from a file: dishes, prices and allergens recognised automatically
  • Rooms and tables generated from the seats, with opening hours and online bookings
  • Ordering from the QR code on the table, guests pay at the restaurant as usual
  • Kitchen, bar and pizza oven in sync, each with its own tablet
  • Waiters and riders on their phones, covers and tips at the end of the month

We can take any Olvix module and adapt it to your business, with your brand and your data.
