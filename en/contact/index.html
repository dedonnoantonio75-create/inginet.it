How quickly do you reply?

Within one working day, usually much sooner. If you message on WhatsApp during office hours you will normally hear back shortly.

Is the first consultation paid?

No. The first meeting and the initial analysis of the problem are free and without obligation. You pay when you decide to start the work, against a written fixed quote.
