Cookie policy

Last updated: September 2026

Here, in plain words, is what this site stores on your device. The short answer: very little, and nothing that profiles you. The panel at the bottom left lets you change your mind at any time.

What we store today

The site sets no cookies of its own and none from third parties. To remember your chosen language, the light or dark theme and your consent decision we use the browser’s local storage: it stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

The consent panel

On your first visit a panel appears at the bottom left with three routes: accept everything, keep only what is necessary, or open “Customise” and decide item by item. After you choose, the panel closes and a small tab remains, still at the bottom left: from there you can reopen it and change your mind whenever you like. A green dot means statistics are on, a grey one that they are off.

Technical and necessary

Language, theme and the memory of your consent. These need no permission because they make the site work and do not profile you in any way.

Anonymous statistics

They would only tell us how many people read the pages. No statistics tool is installed at the moment: the option exists so that your choice already counts if we add one in future. In that case it would start only after your explicit consent, never before.

Profiling and third parties

We use none. No active Google Analytics, no advertising pixels, no embedded maps or videos, no fonts loaded from external servers: the typefaces are hosted on our own servers.

External links

Links to our clients’ sites, to WhatsApp or to social networks open third-party domains that may set their own cookies: in that case their notices apply, not this one.

How to delete everything

Reopen the panel from the tab at the bottom left and change your choice, or clear the site data from your browser’s privacy and security settings. You will only lose the stored language, theme and consent.

Changes to this document

If we ever introduce tools that require consent, this page will be updated and nothing will be installed before you have chosen.
