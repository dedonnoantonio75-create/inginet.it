Cookie policy

Last updated: September 2026

Good news: there is nothing here to accept. This site sets no profiling cookies, uses no third-party analytics and loads no resources from external servers. That is why you see no consent banner.

Technical cookies

The site sets no cookies of its own. To remember the language you chose and your light or dark theme preference we use the browser’s local storage: that information stays on your device, is never sent to us or to anyone else, and you can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

Statistical and profiling cookies

We use none. There is no Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, no embedded maps, no embedded video and no fonts loaded from external servers.

Third-party cookies

None. Links to our clients’ sites, to WhatsApp or to social networks open third-party domains that may set their own cookies: in that case their notices apply, not this one.

How to delete stored data

In any browser you can delete site data from the privacy and security settings. Clearing inginet.it data will only lose your stored language and theme.

Changes to this document

If we ever introduce tools that require consent, this page will be updated and a compliant banner will appear before any non-technical cookie is set.
