Frequently asked questions

What exactly does a software house working with artificial intelligence do?

It designs and writes custom software in which AI performs a precise job: answering customers, reading and classifying documents, drafting quotes, forecasting workload, translating content. Inginet starts from the business process, identifies the repetitive steps and replaces them with an agent trained on the company’s own data and integrated into the software already in use.

How much does a project with Inginet cost?

It depends on what it has to do. A well-indexed multilingual brochure site is one order of magnitude; a custom business system with AI built in is another. The first meeting and the analysis are free: at the end you get a fixed quote with written timelines and no costs that appear halfway through.

Do you only work in southern Italy?

Our office is in Maglie, in the province of Lecce, but we serve clients across Italy and abroad. Analysis, development, training and support all work remotely without losing quality; when meeting in person matters, we meet.

My current site is on WordPress — do we have to redo everything?

Not always. Sometimes it is enough to clean it up, lighten it and secure it. Other times rebuilding on a static foundation is better: faster, safer and cheaper to maintain. We decide after looking at the site, not before.

What does optimising a site for artificial intelligence mean?

It means writing and structuring pages so that models such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google’s AI Overviews can extract precise answers and cite your company as a source. It needs question-and-answer content, structured data, declared entities, an llms.txt file and a clean content hierarchy. It is a different job from classic SEO and it is done in addition, not instead.
