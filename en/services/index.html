Frequently asked questions

How long does a multilingual website take?

For a well-structured, properly indexed brochure site in five languages, usually three to six weeks, depending on how quickly content and photography arrive. E-commerce or management software follow different timelines, which we agree together during the analysis.

Can I update the content myself?

Yes. Depending on the project you get a proprietary CMS, a custom panel or very simple content files. In every case we train you, with a recorded session you can go back to whenever you like.

Does the software remain my property?

Yes. Source code, database and documentation are yours. You can keep them on your servers or ours, and take them away at any time.

Do you take over projects started by someone else?

Often. We analyse what exists, say honestly what is worth keeping and what is not, and continue from there. It is not unusual for the first useful step to be securing and speeding things up rather than rewriting.

Tell us the problem, we’ll tell you how we solve it

Free initial analysis, no strings attached. We reply within one working day, in English, Italian, German, French or Spanish.
